JUST SHIPPED · WEEK 1

"The Floor" — nothing breaks, nothing gets lost, and Art can finally win a fight.

  • Art can win now. Junior damage floor (≥15% per hit) plus an incoming-hit cap — a Lv8 starter beats a gym ace in ~7 hits instead of ~84, and trainer AI stops playing smart against a 4-year-old.
  • Evolution from gym wins restored. The game's biggest XP source was the one place evolution never fired — a one-line bug, now fixed with the punish-move ordering corrected too.
  • One way out of a battle. Single teardown authority + battle-epoch guards; an ESCAPE tap can no longer strip Art's no-faint shield or leak damage into the next battle.
  • Saves are defended. Empty-save wipe refused, ↩️ UNDO IMPORT, catches banked at decision time, quarantine for unreadable saves, element-level import validation.
  • WHO'S PLAYING? picker on first boot — Art no longer lands in Gabe's profile with Junior Mode off.
  • The narrator is gone. All TTS deleted; a permanent smoke-suite guard fails the build if speech ever returns.
  • Service worker actually caches. One wrong check meant zero sprites had ever been cached; also a 2.5s shell timeout so a slow network can't hang boot on a white screen.
  • Global error net + photosensitivity fix. Friendly "something went wobbly" recovery screen, sprite fallbacks, and both seizure-range flashes slowed below threshold (with reduced-motion support).

WHERE WE'RE GOING

Three weeks left in the arc. Each week ships as its own push, in order.

Week 2 — The Team and the World Up next

Make growth felt. The engine extraction lands first — nothing below it ships safely until the old numbers are pinned by tests.

  • engine.js extraction + node test suite — DOM-free battle math (stats, damage, catch odds, XP) shared by battle and catch, which today run two different catch formulas. Gate for everything after it.
  • Party XP share — KO'er 100%, bench 50%. Today 269 battles fund exactly one Pokémon.
  • Gym spoils ceremony — "PICK YOUR FAVORITE" from the beaten roster; whole team still awarded, level-honest, no more silent no-ops.
  • Habitat level bands with a leash — each zone gets real difficulty that rises with badges, clamped around the lead so the volcano can't wall Art.
  • Lead chip + difficulty-dial fix — persistent lead sprite/level in the toolbar slot VOICE freed; wild levels derive from your actual team.
  • Damage honesty — physical/special split (rescues Alakazam & Gengar), variance roll, one-shot cap, move hygiene, Sparkle rescoped to 1.5×.
  • Daily-loop repair — local-time quest rollover (UTC currently wipes progress at dinnertime), meaningful quest XP, and the app's first visible XP bar.

Week 3 — Show, Don't Tell The big swing

A battle screen a pre-reader can read at a glance — no words needed. Today ~100% of the battle narrative is 9px text, and half the audience can't read.

  • Wordless move buttons — big type-coloured tiles with type emoji (🔥💧⚡🌿); this decides whether Art is choosing or mashing.
  • A visual grammar for outcomes — super-effective = red chevron + hard shake + huge number; immune = grey ✖; crit = flash-frame. Sensation replaces sentences.
  • Icon-led status — fainted sprites grey out and tip over; team pips under HP boxes; animated switching.
  • Retire all 21 native dialogs — one icon-led dialog component (native dialogs silently fail in iOS standalone PWAs).
  • Tap-to-skip + celebration pass — cuts ~43s of dead air per gym fight; confetti and fanfare on every level-up.
  • Audio lifecycle + speed — fix iOS context interruption (one phone call currently silences the game until relaunch), prefetch battles, local moves.json for persistent movesets.

Week 4 — The Ending, the Long Tail & What Dad Sees Planned

Close the post-Champion cliff and the collection dead-end — the game currently forgets that a boy became Champion.

  • Hall of Fame — full Champion ceremony, team march-in, permanent 👑 on the trainer card, champion flag persisted.
  • Unlock the 58 dead trainers — rematches, then a Round 2 circuit at +15 levels.
  • Badge rebase — 11 gym badges + a late tier (100/300/649 caught, first shiny, Champion); visible progress instead of tooltips.
  • Habitat backfill + FARAWAY LAND — 336 currently-unplaced species get homes; 52% of the dex is unreachable through play today.
  • Master Ball gets a moment — legendary auto-catch exemption, guaranteed-on-a-legendary framing, pity counter on throws.
  • Parent dashboard behind the PIN — play minutes, last backup age, PIN fails closed (it currently fails open), dangerous buttons moved behind the gate.
  • Last-mile hygiene — self-hosted font + icons, privacy note, 8px type floor.
v20 candidates on deck: seen-gate dex catching ("FIND IT ▶" routing, only after FARAWAY LAND soaks), SEASONS, collectible RULES cards. Explicitly rejected for good: streaks, currencies, flee timers, level caps — nothing that takes things away from a kid.

HARD PRODUCT RULES

The constitution — every design argument tiebreaks to the boys' fun.

🔇
The game does not talk
No TTS, ever. A test guard fails the suite if speech returns. Picture, colour and motion carry meaning.
🛡️
Junior never punishes
Art's Pokémon don't faint and his balls don't miss — and the game never advertises the help. He should feel skilled.
🎁
Never take from a child
No flee timers, no streaks to break, no currency they can't afford, no removing an earned Pokémon.
💾
Saves are sacred
Two boys' collections live in one localStorage key. State changes get extreme care.
🧱
Zero build, zero deps
Vanilla ES modules served as authored on GitHub Pages. No bundler, no CI in front of a weekly push.
