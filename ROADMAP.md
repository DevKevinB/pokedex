# Pokédex OS — 4-Week Roadmap (v19 arc)

> **Revision note (v2):** the original synthesis made a talking Junior Mode the Week-3 big swing. Kevin has ruled that **the game does not talk** — all speech synthesis comes out, including what ships today. The council's underlying CRITICAL finding is unchanged (ART is a pre-reader and ~100% of the battle narrative is text), so the answer becomes **visual instead of vocal**: pictures, colour, motion and icons carry the meaning. This suits the GBA aesthetic better than narration ever did.

## Executive summary
Pokédex OS is an excellent week-one game with a nine-to-twelve-day shelf life and a floor that can drop out from under ART without anyone noticing. Three verified facts set the month: Junior Mode is both unloseable *and* unwinnable (a Lv8 mon needs ~450 turns to beat Champion Rex); one ESCAPE tap in a versus match silently strips ART's invincibility for the rest of the session; and `sw.js:63` means the asset cache has never cached a single sprite. Meanwhile the game's biggest joys — level-up, evolution, becoming Champion — are delivered as 9px text, and evolution can never fire from a gym win at all.

The through-line: **build the floor, widen the drain, show instead of tell, give it an ending.** W1 protects fun (teardown, saves, junior damage, evolution restored) and strips the speech engine out. W2 makes the six-slot team and the world real. W3 is the big swing — a battle screen a pre-reader can *read at a glance* without a single word. W4 closes the post-Champion cliff and the collection dead-end.

Biggest risk if nothing changes: ART cannot finish a gym battle, and GABE runs out of new things around day twelve.

## The verdict in one line
Fix the floor first, then make the game *show* instead of tell — ART should be able to win a fight and understand what just happened without reading a word, and GABE should still have something new to chase in week four.

## Explicitly deferred

- **All speech synthesis — narration, spoken names, the VOICE button (was the W3 big swing; Hassan, Kim, Mendes)** — **removed by product ruling, not deferred.** The game does not talk. Week 3's visual grammar carries the same load for the same audience.
- **Seen-gate on non-junior dex catching (endorsed CRITICAL/L)** — deliberately sequenced to v20. Week 4's habitat backfill is its hard prerequisite; gating first would strand 336 species. Ship it the week *after* FARAWAY LAND has soaked, as "FIND IT ▶" routing plus silhouettes-with-habitat-hints. Junior exempt forever.
- **Flee timers on legendaries/shinies (Economy HIGH, Fun REJECTED)** — the two panels split. I took the half both agree on (legendary auto-catch exemption + guaranteed-Master-Ball framing) and left the timer out: a pre-reader watching a legendary vanish learns the game takes things away.
- **Pick-one-at-trainer-level−3 spoils (Economy CRITICAL, structure)** — kept the whole team per the Fun panel's ruling and shipped only the ceremony + honesty fixes. Revisit if W2's party XP fails to close the raised-vs-looted gap.
- **Poké Coins currency (Fischer)** — rejected by three panels: a new shop, schema and balance surface, and it teaches a 4-year-old "you can't afford that."
- **Level ceiling / sublinear XP damper / quadratic thresholds (Sharma)** — right diagnosis, anti-north-star lever. Never freeze the one number the boy watches.
- **TTK-inverted damage engine (Petrov)** — rewrites the most load-bearing expression in a 979-line file and makes the physical/special split meaningless.
- **Battle-kernel rewrite / phase state machine (Schultz, Tanaka, Carter, Rezai)** — right destination, wrong week. The epoch guard + engine.js extraction buy ~80% of the safety for ~10% of the blast radius.
- **Action journal, ring buffers, checksums, IndexedDB, merge-sync + QR (Webb, Ndiaye, Alvarez, Williams)** — all rewrite the artifact they protect. Two extra localStorage keys close every observed loss path. Ship only the forward-compatible half now: timestamp + per-player catch counts in the export payload.
- **Monte-Carlo balance harness + CI scenario split (Santos)** — measurement isn't the bottleneck; safely changing a formula is. `node --test` over engine.js does that in W2.
- **Full 649-mon build-time bake via GitHub Action (Girma, Moretti, Larsen)** — a CI pipeline in front of a non-developer's weekly push, with a silent stale-data failure mode. Take the artifact (moves.json), not the automation.
- **Retained-mode PC box, sprite-sheet cartridge pack, full Road-Trip prefetch** — relocate real cost into boot, need a Node ritual, or push hundreds of MB into the quota that already evicts.
- **Full WCAG program (Mendes)** — two touch-only users, no keyboard, no screen reader. Carved out and shipped: strobe fix, reduced-motion, sprite alt text. His self-voicing recommendation is now permanently out of scope.
- **Forgiving-tap engine, flick-to-throw, gesture rewrite (Berg, Nair, Park)** — nondeterministic input a non-developer can't reason about when ART says "it did the wrong thing."
- **Two-font system (Lindqvist)** — retuning every screen at once for a legibility win the 8px floor already mostly delivers.
- **Daily streaks / login rewards / streak insurance (Brandt)** — rejected on ethics: the boys don't control device access, so a streak makes them lobby their father and turns bedtime into a loss.
- **Priced heals, gym-entry costs, session caps, removing auto-catch on KO** — attrition and engineered loss in a game whose only currency is a kid's attention.
- **SEASONS / generative 13th gym (Osei), per-child PWA installs (Oduya), scripted cold-open tutorial (Fontaine), collectible RULES cards (Alvarez), salted-SHA-256 PIN (Stone), per-Pokémon leitmotifs (Kowalski)** — premature or out-scoped this month; SEASONS and RULES cards are the strongest v20 candidates once the Champion flag exists.

## Risks to watch

1. **battle.js churn across W1 and W2** (teardown + epoch + damage math in the hottest file). → engine.js and its `node --test` suite land *first in the W2 push*, before any formula edit; W1 guards and W2 math ship as separate weekly diffs so a bad Saturday reverts to a known-good week.
2. **Keeping whole-team gym spoils may leave looted mons out-levelling raised ones.** → Instrument it cheaply: after W2, watch one gym run — if GABE's hand-raised lead still trails his loot at gym 4, ship Economy's pick-one-at-−3 in v20. The ceremony UI is already built for it.
3. **Habitat bands could turn the volcano into a wall for ART.** → Ship the leash clamp ([lead−3,+5] junior) in the same commit as the bands, never after; pips are information, never a lock; the W1 junior damage floor is already underneath it.
4. **The visual grammar is now the *only* channel for a pre-reader — if it doesn't land, nothing replaces it.** → Ship W3 slices 1-2 as one push, then watch ART play a single gym battle unaided: can he tell who won each exchange? If not, the fallback is louder (bigger chevrons, longer hit-stop, a persistent 👍/👎 badge on the HP box), never words.
5. **Removing TTS touches five files plus the smoke suite.** → Do it as its own commit inside W1 with a full suite run after; `stopAllAudio` is called from six sites, so grep `speak(`, `isSpeaking`, `voice-btn` and `speechSynthesis` before declaring it done.
